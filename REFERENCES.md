# Mathematical sources and scope

The references below identify the source problem and the mathematical tools used in the manuscript. This list is not an exhaustive priority survey.

1. **Johann Cigler.** *Some experimental observations about Hankel determinants of convolution powers of Catalan numbers*, [arXiv:2308.07642v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.07642v2), 2023. Definitions (1)–(2), page 1; sign convention (16), and Conjecture 6, equation (19), page 4. This is the exact source of the two-offset degree statement with `j>=1, k>=2j-1`.
2. **Ying Wang and Guoce Xin.** *Hankel determinants for convolution powers of Catalan numbers*, Discrete Mathematics 342 (2019), 2694–2716; [arXiv:1811.00248v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.00248v2). Conjecture 10, page 3, and Theorem 11, page 4. Within Cigler's stated valid range, the finite known j=3 specializations include `k=5,...,15`. The wider printed conjecture range must not be silently substituted for the hypotheses here.
3. **Johann Cigler.** *Catalan numbers, Hankel determinants and Fibonacci polynomials*, [arXiv:1801.05608v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.05608v3), 2018. Conjecture 7.7, equation (7.13), page 30, concerns neighboring formulas. The excluded pair `(k,j)=(4,3)` has degree 6 and lies outside the theorem's range.
4. **Christian Krattenthaler.** *Hankel determinants of linear combinations of moments of orthogonal polynomials, II*, [arXiv:2101.04225v5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.04225v5); [DOI:10.1007/s11139-021-00514-8](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11139-021-00514-8). Theorem 1 and Proposition 5 provide classical moment-modification determinant identities. The manuscript gives the applicable finite-matrix argument with the orthogonality norms equal to 1; no claim is made that the general identity is new.
5. **Guo-Niu Han.** *Hankel Determinants for Convolution Powers of Narayana Polynomials*, Electronic Journal of Combinatorics 33(2) (2026), P2.42; [arXiv:2512.12603v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.12603v1); [DOI:10.37236/14995](https://doi.org/10.37236/14995). Theorems 4–7 treat shifts `-m,1-m,2-m,3-m`. For `m=k>=5` these are not the zero shift considered in the j=3 theorem.
6. **Shane Chern and Wenle Shi.** *Hankel determinants of Catalan-like sequences*, [arXiv:2608.27208v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27208v1), 2026. Theorem 1.4, page 3, specialized to `k_CS=k-1,m=k-1,c=2`, gives the neighboring offset-zero and offset-one formulas. This comparison does not establish worldwide priority for the theorem proved here.
7. **NIST Digital Library of Mathematical Functions.** [Equation 4.22.3](https://dlmf.nist.gov/4.22.E3), cotangent partial fractions, and [equation 4.19.6](https://dlmf.nist.gov/4.19.E6), cotangent Laurent coefficients. These classical formulas supply the positive moment sequence used to establish a nonzero leading coefficient.

The preliminary moment-functional construction, determinant reduction, polynomiality and reflection are retained with proofs and their existing attribution. The uniform degree argument proceeds through the higher-rank quotient minor and the positivity of its leading moment determinant. The supplementary fixed-k certificate is not a premise of the proof. No third-party source PDF is redistributed in this package.
